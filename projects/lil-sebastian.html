Problem

Local government meetings — city council, planning commission, school board — are public record, but almost no one watches them. Hours of dry footage sit unwatched while decisions that affect housing, zoning, and budgets go unnoticed by the people they impact.

Lil Sebastian solves this by turning meeting recordings into short, entertaining podcast episodes. Government workers upload a video; citizens browse and listen by city — no need to sit through raw footage.

The Product

  • Citizen experience

    Browse episodes by city, search for a town, listen to recent meetings, and track recently played — available on mobile (Expo/React Native) and web.

  • Government worker portal

    Upload meeting videos through the website to kick off the automated podcast generation pipeline.

  • AI podcast cast

    Four distinct characters — Narrator, Ben, Leslie, and Patrick — each with their own ElevenLabs voice, turning dry transcripts into conversational episodes.

Pipeline Details

  • Audio conversion

    Meeting videos are converted to M4A via ffmpeg for downstream transcription.

  • Transcription

    ElevenLabs speech-to-text produces a full transcript of the meeting audio.

  • Script generation

    Google Gemini rewrites the transcript into an engaging multi-character podcast script with dialogue, transitions, and humor.

  • Multi-voice synthesis

    A Python pipeline parses the script, assigns voices per character via voice_map.json, and stitches dialogue into a final MP3 using ElevenLabs TTS and pydub.

  • Distribution

    Finished episodes are uploaded to Supabase Storage and surfaced in the app and website for citizens to stream.
