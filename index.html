About

I'm a computer science graduate from UC Davis with experience spanning industry ML systems and academic research. At Advantest, I fine-tuned Llama 3.3 70B and deployed production RAG pipelines for semiconductor test automation. At UC Davis, I co-authored research on LLM bias evaluation and multi-dimensional reasoning model assessment.

I'm passionate about making LLMs reliable in real-world settings — whether that's eliminating hallucinations in hardware spec retrieval or exposing what single-number benchmarks hide about model behavior.
